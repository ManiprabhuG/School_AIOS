# Template.md

# Objective

Design and implement a complete **Printing Template System** for the School Management ERP. Every module that generates printable information must have professionally designed, print-ready templates with consistent branding, responsive layouts, and export capabilities.

The templates should be suitable for:

- A4 Paper
- Letter Size Paper
- Thermal Receipt Printers (58mm & 80mm)
- PDF Export
- Direct Browser Printing

Maintain a modern, professional, and enterprise-grade design throughout all templates.

---

# General Template Standards

All templates must include:

- School Logo
- School Name
- School Address
- Contact Number
- Email Address
- Website (Optional)
- Academic Year
- Document Title
- Unique Document Number
- Generated Date & Time
- Generated By
- QR Code (Optional)
- Barcode Support (Optional)
- Watermark (Optional)
- Authorized Signature Section
- Footer with Page Numbers

The design must be clean, readable, and optimized for both digital and printed formats.
